## Executive Summary

**Overall status: Mixed**

The site presents a clear, modern proposition for a freelance web-development studio. Its homepage describes 1976 London as a freelance web development and creative technology studio run by Stuart Hunt in London, identifies several target client groups, showcases project work and provides clear routes to the Work, Services and Contact pages.

The strongest opportunities are to expand the number and depth of search-focused landing pages, develop detailed case studies, build topical authority, strengthen local search signals and verify core technical controls through a direct crawl and platform access.

No numeric SEO score is assigned because important inputs, including Search Console data, analytics, backlink data, DNS configuration, field performance and a complete crawl, were not available.

## Key Strengths

### 1. Clear positioning

The homepage immediately identifies the business as a freelance web development and creative technology studio in London. This gives visitors and search engines a clear understanding of the site's principal topic.

### 2. Defined audiences

The homepage explicitly mentions artists, photographers, filmmakers, musicians, charities and local businesses. These audiences provide a strong basis for focused service pages and content clusters.

### 3. Portfolio-led trust

Featured projects include a project category, concise description and relevant implementation themes or technologies. This provides useful evidence of practical work and can be developed into richer case studies.

### 4. Simple navigation

The main navigation is concise and understandable: Home, Work, Services and Contact. The homepage also includes clear calls to view work, explore services and contact Stuart.

### 5. Relevant conversion proposition

The copy connects development work to business outcomes, including showcasing work, generating better enquiries and enabling sites to grow without rebuilding from scratch.

# Prioritised Findings

## Critical Priority

### 1. Expand search entry pages

**Evidence**
The visible architecture is concentrated around Home, Work, Services and Contact, while the homepage addresses several distinct audiences and service themes.

**Why it matters**
Dedicated pages can align much more closely with specific search intent than a single broad homepage or general services page.

**Recommended action**

Create focused, substantial landing pages where the offering is genuinely available, potentially including:

- Freelance Web Developer London
- Astro Development Services
- Portfolio Website Design
- Artist Portfolio Websites
- Photographer Website Development
- Filmmaker Portfolio Websites
- Charity Website Development
- Small Business Websites

Each page should have a distinct purpose, original copy, relevant examples, FAQs, internal links and a clear enquiry path. Avoid producing near-duplicate pages solely to target keyword variations.

**Expected outcome**
A broader set of relevant search entry points and clearer alignment between queries, landing pages and services.

**Effort**
Medium

**Dependencies**
Confirmed service offering, keyword research, case-study material and a scalable page template.

**Verification**

- Pages return HTTP 200 and are indexable
- Each page has a self-referencing canonical
- Pages appear in the XML sitemap
- Search Console begins reporting impressions for relevant queries
- Landing-page engagement and enquiries are monitored

### 2. Build topical authority content

**Evidence**
A blog, insights hub or guide library was not identified in the public evidence reviewed.

**Why it matters**
Useful supporting content can answer prospective clients' questions, strengthen internal linking and demonstrate expertise around the site's core services.

**Recommended action**

Create an advice or insights section shaped around real client needs, for example:

- Choosing a website platform for a creative portfolio
- Astro versus WordPress for a portfolio website
- Website performance considerations for image-heavy portfolios
- Preparing content for an artist or photographer website
- Small-business website planning checklist

Content should be based on genuine experience, avoid generic filler and link naturally to relevant services and case studies.

**Expected outcome**
Greater topical depth, more opportunities to appear for research-led searches and stronger paths from informational content to commercial pages.

**Effort**
Medium

**Dependencies**
Editorial ownership, subject expertise, examples and a publishing workflow.

**Verification**

- Articles are crawlable, internally linked and included in the sitemap
- Search Console reports impressions and clicks for non-branded queries
- Assisted enquiries from advice content are tracked

## High Priority

### 3. Verify and strengthen local SEO signals

**Evidence**
The homepage identifies London as the business location and names Stuart Hunt as the person running the studio.

**Why it matters**
Consistent, verifiable local signals help users understand the service area and may improve relevance for location-qualified searches.

**Recommended action**

- Create or optimise a Google Business Profile if the business is eligible
- Keep the business name, location/service area and contact details consistent
- Add an About page with clear London context and genuine credentials
- Add appropriate `LocalBusiness` or `ProfessionalService` structured data only when the represented facts are accurate and visible
- Include location references naturally on relevant service and contact pages

**Expected outcome**
Clearer local relevance, stronger trust and improved eligibility for appropriate local-search experiences.

**Effort**
Low to medium

**Dependencies**
Business eligibility, accurate address or service-area information and profile ownership.

**Verification**

- Validate structured data with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator
- Check business information for consistency across owned profiles
- Monitor Search Console queries containing London or nearby location terms

### 4. Turn project summaries into detailed case studies

**Evidence**
The homepage presents featured projects for an artist portfolio, filmmaker portfolio and small-business website, with concise descriptions and technology or outcome themes.

**Why it matters**
Detailed case studies can provide richer evidence of expertise, create indexable content and help prospects understand the process and value delivered.

**Recommended action**

Give each suitable project its own page covering:

- Client context and objective
- Constraints or challenge
- Approach and implementation
- Accessibility, performance and SEO considerations where relevant
- Before-and-after evidence or verified outcomes
- Client quotation, with permission
- Related service links and a project-specific call to action

Do not claim performance, traffic or commercial results unless measurement supports them.

**Expected outcome**
Stronger trust, more relevant long-tail search coverage and better-qualified enquiries.

**Effort**
Medium

**Dependencies**
Client permission, project assets and reliable outcome evidence.

**Verification**

- Case-study pages are indexed
- Internal links connect projects to related services
- Enquiry journeys from case studies are tracked

### 5. Audit and implement structured data

**Evidence**
Structured-data implementation was not verified from the evidence available in this review.

**Why it matters**
Accurate structured data can help search engines interpret the business, website, person, services and breadcrumb relationships. It does not guarantee enhanced search results.

**Recommended action**

Audit current markup and implement only appropriate, truthful types, potentially including:

- `WebSite`
- `Organization` or an appropriate local business subtype
- `Person`
- `Service`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `CreativeWork` for suitable case-study or portfolio material

**Expected outcome**
Clearer entity and page relationships for search engines and eligibility for supported search features where requirements are met.

**Effort**
Low to medium

**Dependencies**
Accurate business facts and page templates that expose the same information visibly.

**Verification**

- Test representative URLs
- Resolve errors and review warnings
- Monitor Search Console enhancement reports where available

# Verification Steps
